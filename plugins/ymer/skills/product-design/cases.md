# Product design principles: cases

The cases of the product-design skill: what to do when the area cannot
be read, and why the skill leaves the area as it found it. The headings
are `SKILL.md`'s own; read a section's entries at the moment that
section runs.

## The product area

- **When the store does not answer**, defer and retry each turn. A
  read that lands before the verdict counts as run; when none lands,
  the verdict proceeds without the area and names the store that did
  not answer, never "no area" (the fallback rule: *Defaults and
  holds*, the process contract).
- **This skill writes nothing in the area.** Its output is the verdict
  in the direction doc and, for material worth keeping that no settled
  section already holds, **vision** drops in the pool, several in one
  session if need be (grammar: the `capture` skill; drawing: the `mint`
  skill). A product's vision drops are drawn into a **carve topic**,
  and content reaches the area that way, never through this skill. A
  drop for a product with no area waits until its owner creates that
  product's area.
- **The area has no collection section,** deliberately, and no trigger
  that drains one: **intake is the pool**, and collected material rides
  the one intake that already exists. A proposal to give the area a
  landing section, a staging section, or a threshold that fires a drain
  is refused on those grounds, whatever it is called.
