# Product design principles: cases

The cases of the product-design skill: what to do when the page cannot
be read, and why the skill leaves the page as it found it. The headings
are `SKILL.md`'s own; read a section's entries at the moment that
section runs.

## The product page

- **When the store does not answer**, defer and retry each turn. A
  fetch that lands before the verdict counts as run; when none lands,
  the verdict proceeds without the page and names the store that did
  not answer, never "no page" (the fallback rule: *Defaults and
  holds*, development-process).
- **This skill writes nothing on the page.** Its output is the verdict
  in the direction doc and, for material worth keeping that no settled
  section already holds, **vision** drops in the pool, several in one
  session if need be (grammar: the `capture` skill; drawing: the `mint`
  skill). Mint drains a product's vision drops as a **carve topic**,
  and content reaches the page that way, never through this skill. A
  drop for a product with no page waits until that
  product's onboarding creates one.
- **The page has no collection area,** deliberately, and no trigger
  that drains one: **intake is the pool**, and collected material rides
  the one intake that already exists. A proposal to give the page a
  landing area, a staging section, or a threshold that fires a drain is
  refused on those grounds, whatever it is called.
