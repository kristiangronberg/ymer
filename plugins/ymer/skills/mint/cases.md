# Mint — evidence discipline at the intake boundary — cases

The cases of the mint skill, moved here byte for byte from `SKILL.md`, which keeps the intent and the spine and names this file under every heading that has entries here. The headings are `SKILL.md`'s own; read a section's entries at the moment that section runs.

## Three looks, mandatory at every mint

**The already-ran rule:** a look satisfied by a step the session
already ran counts as done and is never re-run; a look no step ran is
owed.

## The register on the item's evidence

The item's evidence rides the claims register (register rules: the
sweep skill's Claims section):

- **Content claims ride probe-or-mark.** Mechanisms, consumer-side
  steps in adoption intake, sizing counts, symptom traces: each
  carries its probe (a stamp) or the literal marker `inferred:` —
  synthesis and estimation named as what they are, never dressed as
  observation. **What a number implies is a second claim.** A stamp's
  characterization — what the result means, why it came out that way,
  what must therefore be true — is not carried by the stamp: probe it or
  mark it `inferred:` in its own right (the sweep skill's "a stamp's
  authority ends at its output"). A producer who sees a measured number
  and stops there hands the reading forward as if it had been measured.
- **Sizing counts are measured claims.** A count that will size the
  topic is a stamped sweep, or it is marked unswept — the consuming
  brainstorm re-derives either way, and the mark is what tells it to.
- **Cited claims carry their basis.** A fact reused from elsewhere is
  re-verified or carries `as-of <basis>`; citing an inferred claim
  keeps its mark — the mark travels.
- **A handed-forward question names its ground.** An item that asks
  the consumer to verify something names where the evidence lives —
  the store, the environment, prod versus dev — or says it doesn't
  know. "Doesn't know" is a legal answer; a fabricated pointer is
  not.
- **A citation resolves as-is.** An item named in the evidence is cited
  in a form the coordinator resolves without a listing — the compound
  `name-<uuid>` by preference, a full UUID at least, never a UUID
  prefix, which no read action takes and which cost two 100-item pool
  listings to expand.
- **The name keeps to the observation.** An item's name states what was
  seen; the inferred mechanism rides the body with its mark. The name
  is the one line every reader takes as given, and a mechanism stated
  there as fact framed nine annotators for four weeks.

## The triage lead

A risk-call item — one whose lead asks whether a risk is acceptable —
carries two more slots in that lead: **how often** and **how bad**, each
stamped or marked `inferred:`. A forced reproduction is a reachability
stamp, not a likelihood, and an item carrying neither hands the consumer
the sizing.

## Annotating

An annotate whose evidence refutes or narrows the item's claim
**re-leads the name** to the live residual and keeps the old title in
the body: *list inbox items* and triage read the name, and a stale one invites
the wrong brainstorm.

**Retiring and folding.** A pass that closes or folds items searches the
open pool's descriptions for each closed item's name (*search pool*, by
subject) and annotates the replacement on every hit — a closed item
cited with no replacement named is a pointer the next brainstorm
rebuilds a frame on.
