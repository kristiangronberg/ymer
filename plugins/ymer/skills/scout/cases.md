# Scout: cases

The cases of the scout skill: the earned precision behind `SKILL.md`'s moments, under the spine's own headings. Read a section's entries at the moment that section runs.

## Overview

The map is a set of leads with a shelf life, not a contract:
write-plan's grounding re-verifies its plan-relevant claims fresh at
plan time. Coverage of concepts therefore beats precision of anchors:
do not collect line-level anchors or exact signatures here. A prose
corpus takes this rule its own way (→ The Survey Map).

## Input

The one carve-out is the phase that invoked scout in-session: define
runs scout when no `survey.md` exists (the define skill, § Input,
step 2), and a live marker naming define is then the caller's brief,
not scout's stop.
The survey proceeds, and the marker stays the caller's to consume. In
that fused opening the vocabulary lens reads the project glossary, and
where the lens ran in this session's own context rather than as a
subagent, define's own glossary read (the define skill, § Input,
step 3) then finds it already read.

## The Survey

The brief's **Verified state** is cited, never re-measured, whichever
path runs. The clause is the survey's own, not the fan-out's: a
small-area survey that searches directly inherits it unchanged. It
covers **stamped** claims only: an `inferred:` line the direction rests
on, wherever it sits in the brief, that section included, is a claim to
re-verify (the sweep skill, § Claims: stamps, controls and the sweep
artifact), and probing it is scout's, before define's interview builds
on it. Where the probe is a script, it is kept beside the map as a
committed behavioral probe.

**Partition the work, not only the questions.** A search vector goes to
exactly one lens, and a corpus several lenses share gets one **measuring
lens**: the others cite its numbers rather than deriving their own. Size
each lens net of what the host session must read itself: a lens that
re-reads what you are reading anyway buys nothing. Recall-first is a
sweep's discipline (the sweep skill), where redundancy buys coverage; in
fan-out design it only multiplies tool work.

Agents return compact fact sheets: paths with one-line roles, terms with
where they are defined, claims quoted with file references, and any
unprobed characterization (what a module is for, why a pattern holds)
marked `inferred:`, never asserted as fact. They gather facts only: no
design proposals, no coding-standards reading. You stay single-context
and synthesize; if the sheets expose an area nobody covered, run one
targeted follow-up, since the map is a survey, not an audit. The map is
the compaction boundary and cites no sheet: anything a later phase
needs is in it. A sheet written to the topic folder is gathered
material that rides the phase commit, and no later phase reads it.

## The Survey Map

In a **prose corpus** (a suite or meta topic, where what the survey maps
is written pages rather than code) the **section is the unit**: quoted
text governs, and a `:line` ref beside it is an authoring-time lead,
never the anchor. That is the altitude rule's form here, not an
exemption from it.
