# Brainstorm: cases

The cases of the brainstorm skill: the earned precision behind `SKILL.md`'s moments, under the spine's own headings. Read a section's entries at the moment that section runs.

## Contents

- Topic Resolution: the discovered set's one question
- The Interview: the hold, probes, evidence sections, sweep lists and sweep artifacts
- Product alignment: its timing, vision drops, tokens bound for another repo
- Scratch Doc → Consolidate: when consolidation starts, a site list's altitude

## Topic Resolution

One idea often surfaces in several places at once: a drawn topic, a
topic directory, a sketch branch. Take everything the lookup found in as
one discovered set, confirmed with a single question: "Found: drawn
topic `<name>`, current branch `<branch>`. All this topic?" On yes, take
all of it in, the drawn topic by § Drawn-topic Intake and the branch by
§ Branch Intake, never piece by piece. At a split child, an iteration or
a pending interview no confirmation fires, and on an iteration the
branch is the reviewed increment rather than a sketch.

A drawn topic adds no question; a near match, a clean miss and a
discovered set add one at most. Topic resolution is a lookup, not an
interview.

## The Interview

**Hold: every question** (the process contract, § Defaults and holds).
Unattended at a question, the phase prepares every remaining question
into `brainstorm.md`'s `## Interview` under the `Interview pending —`
marker and closes around the interview as a pending step (the interview
procedure, § 4. Pending; brainstorm's own close: `SKILL.md` § Unattended:
closing around the interview).

- A probe follows scout's rule for live machine state (the scout skill,
  § The Survey).
- Focus on purpose, constraints, and success criteria: what problem this
  solves and how we would know it worked.

**Evidence sections carry measured claims.** The verified-state section
is optional. When present its heading opens with **"Verified state"**,
with the canonical tail "— what the next phase should not re-derive",
and every claim in it is a measured claim under the sweep skill's
§ Claims: a stamp on each (`command` → observed result), and a
`control:` clause on every absence or zero. The section's charter is why
the duty is strict: what it asserts, the next phase will not re-derive.

**Sweep lists are recall-first.** When the doc enumerates sites (every
file using a term, every place a concept touches), that list is a sweep:
build it by the sweep skill, with substring and stem matching, the whole
corpus, and synonym terms for a meaning sweep, never an exact-word list
of the expected places.

Counts reported for a stem or term family come from a **committed sweep
artifact** in the topic folder, even where the family was first swept
by hand: a hand-run `rg -c` with a printing control stamps a single
measurement, never a family. The artifact rides the phase commit.

A probe or sweep script a later gate is told to **re-run** is committed
beside the doc as well. A behavioral probe's claims ride the ordinary
stamp and control rules, but its script survives all the same: a recipe
restated in prose was rebuilt from the prose twice, wrong on the first
try both times.

### Product alignment

The beat runs at convergence, against a **formed candidate direction**:
after ideation, before the user declares the direction settled. The
moment itself is anchored in this file's § Scratch Doc → Consolidate,
which says when the convergence beats run against the candidate and
what happens when the pick arrives fused with it. Never during divergence:
evaluation criteria during ideation clamp the divergence a brainstorm
exists for.

**The beat writes nothing on the page.** Material worth keeping that no
settled section already holds leaves the session as **vision** drops in
the pool, through the `ymer:capture` skill, several per capture if need
be; the drop grammar is capture's, and the drawing mint's. The drop is
the durable hand-off, so writing one is not optional because the
direction doc also records it.

A new token destined for another repo's vocabulary (a capability
grammar, a cross-repo name) is checked against that repo's glossary
`_Avoid_` bans before it rides a vision drop, a page write or an
annotation: the minting moment is the last one with no gate before the
word propagates, and a banned shape once reached two stores.

## Scratch Doc → Consolidate

**Consolidate only when the user says the direction is settled**, never
before. The convergence beats are what "settled" waits on: they run
against the formed candidate *before* it is offered for the pick, and
where the pick arrives fused with the candidate (an analysis-then-
acknowledge turn, or blanket acceptance of a first proposal), before
that pick is acknowledged. Consolidation starts only after their
verdicts, read as the *convergence beat* entry reads them (term: the
plugin glossary).

The verified-state section's contract, and the rules for sweep lists
and committed sweep artifacts, are `cases.md` § The Interview's: the
probes they govern run there.

A site list the doc carries is written at the survey map's altitude
(the scout skill, `cases.md` § The Survey Map), never pinned to line
numbers. Write-plan's grounding re-verifies line anchors fresh
regardless, so an edit-site list pinned to line numbers is write-plan's
product, and a brainstorm that carries one prices it twice.
